# Code of Conduct

We want The Free Video Editor to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their
experience, background or identity. This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is what we expect in practice.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind. Assume good intentions; tone is easy to lose in writing.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Review the work, not the person.
- Welcome beginners. Explain, do not belittle.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests, and keep discussion public unless it is sensitive.
- Respect maintainers' decisions. You are welcome to disagree and to fork.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers at **contact@beestack.in**. Reports are handled privately and
fairly. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and temporarily or permanently
ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor
Covenant.
